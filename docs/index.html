What you are looking at

The rhizosphere is the narrow soil sleeve under the influence of living roots. Exudates feed and recruit microbes; symbionts deliver nitrogen and phosphorus; decomposers recycle necromass; pathogens probe for weakness. This scene keeps one cross-section in focus rather than spreading thin across many screens.
