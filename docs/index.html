The root–soil continuum

The rhizosphere is the narrow sleeve of soil under the chemical influence of living roots. Exudates recruit and feed microbes; nitrogen-fixing rhizobia and Bradyrhizobium colonize nodules; arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi trade phosphate for carbon; saprotrophs remineralize necromass at the bulk margin; pathogens such as Fusarium probe for weakness; biofilms on the rhizoplane coordinate via quorum sensing. This study keeps one cross-section in focus.
